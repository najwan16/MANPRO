# Business Model — Pay-per-Simulation Hypothesis

## Target jobs-to-be-done

"Saya tertarik melamar System Analyst tetapi belum tahu apakah mampu mengerjakan tugasnya. Saya ingin mencoba aktivitas mirip pekerjaan, melihat kekurangan, dan menghasilkan bukti latihan yang relevan." This is distinct from job posting discovery.

## Value exchange

- **Free:** one authentic structured workplace task, full assessment and feedback on tested decisions; no paywall on result.
- **Paid unit:** one case with three interconnected tasks, generated deliverables, rubric, V1/V2 revision, report, exportable practice portfolio, conditional demo completion certificate.
- **Model:** one-time Pay-per-Simulation; **not a subscription** and not a separate full freemium course. Any later skill pack is a separate purchase only when real differentiated content exists.

## Pricing hypothesis

System Analyst Rp19.000 is a **test price**, not validated willingness-to-pay. Other catalog cards display conceptual future pricing but do not accept purchase.

## Why user might prefer this (hypothesis)

Personal relevance to targeted role, concrete artifact + feedback, ability to revise before applying, access without batch; not guaranteed unique against Rakamin. Competitor response or imitation possible. Content and rubric practitioner review are required to maintain differentiation.

## Example unit economics — Not real figures

| Item | Illustration |
|---|---:|
| Revenue per purchase | Rp19.000 |
| Variable/payment/service cost | Rp3.000 |
| Acquisition cost per buyer | Rp6.000 |
| Contribution before fixed/content cost | Rp10.000 |

Do not present demo checkouts as revenue. Validate acquisition cost, actual conversion, content development, ongoing review, refund, support and repeated purchase.

## Metrics to validate

- Landing → free task start rate.
- Free task complete rate, time and comprehension of feedback.
- Free result → offer detail → checkout interest (demo intent; not purchase).
- Actual payment conversion after safe real deployment and voluntary pricing research.
- Paid task completion, V1/V2 change and transfer to unseen follow-up case.
- Follow-up relevance ratings by students/practitioners, not fake testimonials.

## Five useful investor questions

1. What fraction of users complete the free task before seeing an offer?
2. What genuine result justifies a Rp19k purchase versus free alternatives?
3. Who validated that skills/rubric map to System Analyst work in Indonesia?
4. What is the marginal cost and acquisition cost for one additional paying customer?
5. How do we measure capability transfer rather than scoring the same scenario after hints?
